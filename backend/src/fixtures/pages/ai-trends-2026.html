Five AI trends shaping 2026

Artificial intelligence moved from pilot projects to production in 2026. First, agentic assistants that call tools on a user's behalf became common in operations teams, handling scheduling, reporting and routine data lookups.

Second, smaller domain-tuned models gained ground as companies looked to cut inference costs. Third, regulators in several countries published guidance on transparency and audit logging for automated decisions. Fourth, multimodal models made document processing far more reliable, from invoices to handwritten forms.

Finally, security moved to the centre of the conversation. As agents gained the ability to send email and move money, researchers warned that untrusted web content could steer an agent into actions its user never asked for. Defensive gateways that check every tool call are emerging as a practical answer.
